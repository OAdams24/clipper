Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Clipper is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. It has no servers, no user accounts of its own, and no analytics or advertising.

1. Information Clipper uses

  • TikTok: when you sign in with TikTok, Clipper receives an access token and your basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, to show which account is connected. It uses the video.upload and video.publish scopes only to upload videos you have approved to your own account.
  • YouTube and Instagram: similar access tokens, used only to upload videos you approve to your own accounts.
  • Twitch: public information about streams and clips, such as titles, view counts and chat messages in public channels, used to find highlight moments.
  • Videos: clips and the videos Clipper makes are stored in folders on your computer.

2. Where it is stored

All of this stays on your computer, in Clipper's own folder. Access tokens are stored locally and are only sent to the platform they belong to (for example, the TikTok token is only sent to TikTok). Clipper does not send your information to the app's author or to any other third party.

3. How long it is kept

Information is kept until you remove it. Clipper deletes temporary recordings automatically, and deletes a video's working files when you reject or post it.

4. Your choices

  • Disconnect TikTok at any time in Clipper's settings, or remove Clipper's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions.
  • Delete all of Clipper's data by deleting its folder from your computer.

5. Children

Clipper is not directed at children under 13 and should not be used by them.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
