Clipper

Clipper is a desktop app for Windows that helps a creator turn highlights from Twitch streams into vertical short videos and share them to their own accounts.

What it does

  • Finds highlight moments in Twitch streams the creator follows, with the streamer's permission or as part of a clipping campaign.
  • Edits each moment into a 9:16 vertical video with the facecam on top, gameplay below, burned-in captions and a credit to the streamer.
  • Puts every video in a review queue. Nothing is shared until the creator has reviewed it and approved it.
  • Shares approved videos to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels accounts, or schedules them for later.

How Clipper uses TikTok

The creator signs in with their own TikTok account using Login Kit. Clipper shows which account is connected. When the creator approves a video, Clipper uploads it to that account with the Content Posting API: either to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft, where they finish and post it in the TikTok app, or, if the creator chooses, posted directly with the privacy and interaction settings they pick on Clipper's posting screen.

Clipper runs on the creator's own computer. It has no servers and does not collect data from other people.
